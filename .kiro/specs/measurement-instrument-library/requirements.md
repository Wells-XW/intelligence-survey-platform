# Requirements Document

## Introduction

T-Measurement ("Measurement Instrument Library", 测量工具库) is the substantive, evidence-backed alternative to letting an AI generate survey items from scratch. Instead of inventing items, the Intelligence Survey Platform ingests existing questionnaires from established Chinese and international surveys, extracts every question and its answer options, standardizes each item into a canonical schema, has an AI analyze which concept the item measures and which validated scale family it belongs to, and stores the result in a queryable item bank. The bank then powers the platform's AI-assisted survey designer: when a researcher composes a new survey, the assistant retrieves, recommends, and adapts validated items from the bank with full citation back to the source questionnaire.

This feature elaborates Phase 3 task `task-1778697793535-z59yy3` ("测量工具箱开发", P3, due 2027-06-01) of the Intelligence Survey Platform project. It is delivered as a new microservice within the existing FastAPI backend, a Celery worker pool for ingestion and AI tagging, PostgreSQL tables for canonical item storage, an Elasticsearch index for retrieval, and a small set of UI surfaces embedded in the existing platform: an upload and review console for librarians, an item detail and lineage view for researchers, and a recommendation panel inside the existing AI survey designer.

The initial coverage target is the questionnaire corpus already on disk at the configured library path, covering at minimum CGSS, CFPS, CHARLS, CLHLS, CHIP, CHFS, CHNS, CSS, CLDS, CLASS, CTUS, EASS, ISSP, GSS, and WVS. The feature explicitly excludes raw microdata ingestion (only questionnaire instruments are ingested), automatic translation of items into target languages without human review, and any export of source questionnaire material classified as Rights Tier R2 or R3 outside the local processing pipeline.

## Glossary

- **Survey_Platform**: The Intelligence Survey Platform backend (FastAPI service) that owns survey design, response collection, distribution, and compliance domains, and within which the Measurement_Library is delivered as a subsystem.
- **Measurement_Library**: The subsystem introduced by this feature; comprises the Ingestion_Service, the Item_Extractor, the Standardization_Service, the Concept_Tagger, the Pretty_Printer, the Review_Queue, the Item_Store, and the Recommendation_Service.
- **Source_Questionnaire**: A single questionnaire instrument from a named survey program at a specific wave or year, supplied to the Measurement_Library as one or more files in PDF, Word, or Excel format.
- **Survey_Program**: A named long-running survey effort that publishes one or more Source_Questionnaires; examples include CGSS, CFPS, GSS, ISSP, WVS.
- **Wave**: A specific data collection round of a Survey_Program, identified by year and optional sub-label such as "居民" or "家户" or "电话调查".
- **Ingestion_Service**: The Measurement_Library component that accepts uploaded files, classifies them, runs OCR when needed, and produces a normalized text representation of each Source_Questionnaire.
- **Item_Extractor**: The Measurement_Library component that parses the normalized text representation into a sequence of Raw_Item records, each containing a question stem, an option set, a question type, and any skip logic associated with the question.
- **Raw_Item**: The minimally structured representation of a single question produced by the Item_Extractor, prior to standardization and tagging.
- **Standardization_Service**: The Measurement_Library component that converts a Raw_Item into a Standardized_Item conforming to the canonical schema.
- **Standardized_Item**: The canonical record persisted in the Item_Store; carries fields for stem text, option list, scale anchors, scoring direction, language, source Survey_Program, source Wave, source page number, instrument family, status, version, and Provenance_Record.
- **Question_Type**: A classification label for a Standardized_Item drawn from the closed set {single_choice, multi_choice, likert, numeric, open_text, ranking, matrix, branching}.
- **Skip_Logic**: A machine-readable representation of routing rules attached to a Standardized_Item, expressing for each option which other items are shown or hidden.
- **Concept_Tagger**: The Measurement_Library component that analyzes a Standardized_Item using the platform's multi-model AI router and assigns one or more Concept_Tags and at most one Validated_Scale link.
- **Concept_Tag**: A label drawn from the platform's evolving Concept_Ontology that names the construct an item is intended to measure, such as "subjective_well_being" or "perceived_social_support".
- **Concept_Ontology**: A versioned controlled vocabulary of concepts maintained inside the Measurement_Library; each entry has a stable identifier, a human-readable label in Chinese and English, and a parent-concept reference where applicable.
- **Validated_Scale**: A published, peer-reviewed measurement instrument identified by name and citation, such as the Rosenberg Self-Esteem Scale, GHQ-12, SF-36, or 中国版 PHQ-9; a Standardized_Item may be linked to at most one Validated_Scale.
- **Confidence_Score**: A real number in the closed interval [0.0, 1.0] attached by the Concept_Tagger to each assigned Concept_Tag and to each Validated_Scale link.
- **Pretty_Printer**: The Measurement_Library component that serializes a Standardized_Item back into a human-readable rendering of a questionnaire item, used both for review display and for the round-trip property check.
- **Review_Queue**: The human-in-the-loop surface that lists Standardized_Items awaiting librarian approval, edit, or rejection.
- **Librarian**: A platform user holding the `measurement:review` role, authorized to approve, edit, reject, or merge Standardized_Items in the Review_Queue.
- **Provenance_Record**: A structured record attached to every Standardized_Item that names the Source_Questionnaire file, the page number, the extraction job identifier, the extraction model identifier, and the librarian identity that approved the item.
- **Item_Store**: The persistence surface for the Measurement_Library, consisting of authoritative tables in PostgreSQL and a derived index in Elasticsearch.
- **Recommendation_Service**: The Measurement_Library component that exposes search, retrieval, and recommendation endpoints to the AI survey designer and to the public API.
- **Survey_Designer**: The existing platform surface where a researcher composes a new survey; in this feature it is extended with a recommendation panel that calls the Recommendation_Service.
- **Library_Snapshot**: An immutable, named copy of the Item_Store at a point in time, identified by the snapshot identifier defined in the data compliance steering rules.
- **Rights_Tier**: One of {R0, R1, R2, R3} as defined by the project data compliance rules; R0 and R1 derived item-level metadata may be exposed externally, R2 derived statistics only, R3 internal analysis only.
- **Rights_Tier_Gate**: The Measurement_Library component that classifies every Source_Questionnaire on intake, attaches the resulting Rights_Tier to all derived records, and enforces the export and recommendation rules associated with that tier.
- **Audit_Log**: The append-only persistence surface defined by the existing T10 compliance module of the Survey_Platform; the Measurement_Library writes to the same table, extended with the five mandatory audit fields named below.
- **Data_Snapshot_Id**: The mandatory snapshot identifier in the form `snapshot_YYYYMMDD_NNN`, attached to every Library_Snapshot and to every Audit_Log entry the Measurement_Library writes.
- **Extraction_Version**: The semantic version string of the Item_Extractor that produced a Standardized_Item.
- **Norm_Version**: The semantic version string of the Standardization_Service ruleset that produced the canonical fields of a Standardized_Item.
- **Threshold_Version**: The semantic version string of the Concept_Tagger thresholds (confidence cutoffs, ontology revision) in force when a Standardized_Item was tagged.
- **Trace_Id**: A UUID assigned to each ingestion, tagging, recommendation, and review action, written into both the action's primary record and the corresponding Audit_Log entry.

## Requirements

### Requirement 1: Source Questionnaire Ingestion

**User Story:** As a librarian seeding the item bank, I want to upload existing questionnaires in their original published formats and have the platform recognize each Wave of each Survey_Program, so that I do not have to manually pre-process the corpus already published by major survey projects.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN a librarian uploads a batch of 1 to 50 files where each file has extension `.pdf`, `.docx`, `.doc`, `.xlsx`, or `.xls` and each file size is at most 200 megabytes, THE Ingestion_Service SHALL accept the upload, persist each file under project storage keyed by a SHA-256 content hash, and create one Source_Questionnaire record per file within 30 seconds per file at the 95th percentile.
2. WHEN the Ingestion_Service persists a Source_Questionnaire record, THE Ingestion_Service SHALL store the originating Survey_Program identifier of 1 to 64 characters, the Wave label of 1 to 64 characters, the source language as an ISO 639-1 two-letter code, the upload timestamp in ISO 8601 UTC with millisecond precision, the librarian identifier, the SHA-256 content hash, and the file storage location.
3. IF a librarian attempts to upload a file whose content hash matches an existing Source_Questionnaire record that is not in status `deleted`, THEN THE Ingestion_Service SHALL reject the upload with HTTP status 409 and a machine-readable error code `questionnaire_already_ingested` that includes the existing Source_Questionnaire identifier, and SHALL NOT modify the existing record.
4. IF a librarian uploads a file whose extension is not in the supported set, THEN THE Ingestion_Service SHALL reject the upload with HTTP status 415 and a machine-readable error code `questionnaire_format_unsupported`, and SHALL NOT create any Source_Questionnaire record.
5. WHEN the Ingestion_Service detects that an uploaded PDF contains no embedded text layer or that the embedded text layer covers fewer than 5 percent of pages, THE Ingestion_Service SHALL run OCR on the PDF and SHALL store the OCR output as the normalized text representation alongside the original file.
6. WHEN OCR is run on a Source_Questionnaire, THE Ingestion_Service SHALL record the OCR engine identifier, the OCR engine version, the per-page mean confidence score in the closed interval [0.00, 1.00], the page-level confidence histogram with bin width 0.05, the total OCR runtime in milliseconds, and the OCR completion timestamp in ISO 8601 UTC.
7. IF OCR mean document confidence on a Source_Questionnaire is below 0.80, THEN THE Ingestion_Service SHALL flag the Source_Questionnaire with status `ocr_low_confidence`, SHALL surface the page-level confidence histogram to the Review_Queue, and SHALL block the Source_Questionnaire from advancing to extraction until a librarian explicitly clears the flag.
8. WHEN the librarian provides Survey_Program and Wave metadata at upload time, THE Ingestion_Service SHALL use the librarian-provided values verbatim and SHALL NOT overwrite them with values inferred from filename heuristics.
9. WHERE the librarian does not provide Survey_Program or Wave metadata at upload time, THE Ingestion_Service SHALL infer up to three ranked candidate values per field from the filename and from text recognized on the first three pages, SHALL set the Source_Questionnaire status to `awaiting_metadata_confirmation`, SHALL present the candidates to the librarian for confirmation, and SHALL NOT proceed to extraction until the librarian confirms or corrects the candidates.
10. IF a librarian uploads a batch in which any file exceeds the 200 megabyte cap or the batch exceeds 50 files, THEN THE Ingestion_Service SHALL reject the entire batch with HTTP status 413 and a machine-readable error code `questionnaire_payload_oversize` that names the failing constraint, and SHALL NOT create any Source_Questionnaire record from the rejected batch.
11. IF OCR on a Source_Questionnaire fails to complete within 600 seconds or the OCR engine returns an unrecoverable error, THEN THE Ingestion_Service SHALL set the Source_Questionnaire status to `ocr_failed`, SHALL record the failure category and OCR engine error message of at most 1000 characters, and SHALL retain the original uploaded file and Source_Questionnaire record without partial OCR output.
12. IF a Source_Questionnaire remains in status `awaiting_metadata_confirmation` for more than 14 calendar days without librarian action, THEN THE Ingestion_Service SHALL transition the Source_Questionnaire to status `metadata_expired`, SHALL emit an audit event naming the Source_Questionnaire identifier, and SHALL exclude the Source_Questionnaire from extraction until a librarian re-confirms metadata.

### Requirement 2: Item Extraction from Normalized Text

**User Story:** As a librarian seeding the item bank, I want each ingested questionnaire automatically broken into its individual questions, options, types, and routing rules, so that I review structured items rather than re-reading entire questionnaires.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN a Source_Questionnaire reaches status `ready_for_extraction`, THE Item_Extractor SHALL parse its normalized text representation into a sequence of Raw_Item records ordered by the questions' first character offset in the normalized text in ascending order, and SHALL complete the parse within 60 seconds for any Source_Questionnaire of up to 500 questions.
2. WHEN producing a Raw_Item, THE Item_Extractor SHALL populate the question stem of 1 to 4000 characters, the ordered option list of 0 to 50 options where each option carries a non-empty label of 1 to 1000 characters and a code unique within that Raw_Item, the Question_Type, the source page number as a positive integer, and the source character offset range expressed as inclusive start and exclusive end indices into the normalized text where start is less than end.
3. THE Item_Extractor SHALL classify each Raw_Item's Question_Type as exactly one value from the closed set {single_choice, multi_choice, likert, numeric, open_text, ranking, matrix, branching}.
4. WHEN the Item_Extractor encounters routing or skip instructions associated with an item, THE Item_Extractor SHALL extract those instructions into the Skip_Logic field of the affected Raw_Item, naming target item identifiers that resolve to Raw_Items emitted from the same Source_Questionnaire within the same extraction job.
5. IF a routing or skip instruction names a target identifier that does not resolve to a Raw_Item within the same Source_Questionnaire, THEN THE Item_Extractor SHALL retain the unresolved reference verbatim in the Skip_Logic field, SHALL mark the affected Raw_Item with status `extraction_uncertain`, and SHALL route the Raw_Item to the Review_Queue without aborting the extraction job.
6. WHEN the Item_Extractor encounters a matrix question that presents multiple sub-questions sharing a common option scale, THE Item_Extractor SHALL emit one Raw_Item per sub-question and SHALL preserve a matrix grouping identifier that is identical across every Raw_Item belonging to that matrix and distinct from grouping identifiers of other matrices within the same Source_Questionnaire.
7. IF the Item_Extractor cannot assign a single Question_Type with a confidence score of at least 0.80 on a 0.00 to 1.00 scale, THEN THE Item_Extractor SHALL mark the Raw_Item with status `extraction_uncertain`, SHALL record up to five alternative classifications considered along with their confidence scores, and SHALL route the Raw_Item to the Review_Queue without blocking the rest of the extraction job.
8. WHEN the Item_Extractor finishes processing a Source_Questionnaire, THE Item_Extractor SHALL record the total Raw_Item count, the count per Question_Type for each of the eight values in the closed set including zero counts, the count of Raw_Items routed to the Review_Queue, and the wall-clock duration of the extraction job in milliseconds.
9. THE Item_Extractor SHALL preserve the original wording of every question stem and every option label byte-for-byte without altering punctuation, whitespace, or character width before downstream standardization runs.
10. IF parsing of the normalized text fails such that no Raw_Item can be produced for a Source_Questionnaire, THEN THE Item_Extractor SHALL leave the Source_Questionnaire status unchanged from `ready_for_extraction`, SHALL emit a failure record indicating the failure category and the character offset at which parsing halted, and SHALL not persist any partial Raw_Item records from the failed job.

### Requirement 3: Item Standardization to Canonical Schema

**User Story:** As a researcher reusing items across surveys, I want every item stored in a single canonical shape regardless of its source format, so that I can compare items across CGSS, GSS, and WVS without writing custom adapters each time.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN a Raw_Item reaches the Standardization_Service, THE Standardization_Service SHALL produce a Standardized_Item that contains the canonical stem text of 1 to 2000 characters, the canonical option list of 0 to 50 options, the scale anchor labels, the scoring direction, the source language code expressed as an ISO 639-1 two-letter code, the Survey_Program identifier, the Wave identifier, the source page number as a positive integer, the instrument family identifier, the version number, and the Provenance_Record.
2. THE Standardization_Service SHALL represent every option of a Standardized_Item with a canonical code that remains unchanged across all subsequent versions of the same item, the original source code, the canonical option label of 1 to 500 characters, and the original source label of 1 to 500 characters, even when the canonical code equals the source code.
3. WHEN the Standardization_Service processes a Raw_Item whose Question_Type is `likert`, THE Standardization_Service SHALL record the number of scale points as an integer between 2 and 11, the anchor label at each end of the scale as 1 to 200 characters, and the scoring direction as either `low_to_high` or `high_to_low`.
4. WHEN the Standardization_Service produces a Standardized_Item that has no prior version recorded in the Item_Store for the same instrument family identifier, THE Standardization_Service SHALL set the item version to `1`.
5. WHEN the Review_Queue records an approved edit to an existing Standardized_Item, THE Standardization_Service SHALL set the new item version to one greater than the highest approved version of that item already in the Item_Store.
6. WHEN the Standardization_Service produces a Standardized_Item, THE Standardization_Service SHALL attach a Provenance_Record naming the Source_Questionnaire identifier, the source page number, the extraction job identifier, the Extraction_Version, the Norm_Version in force, and the Trace_Id of the standardization action.
7. IF the Standardization_Service cannot map any of the canonical stem text, the canonical option list, the scoring direction, or the source language code from a Raw_Item to the canonical schema, THEN THE Standardization_Service SHALL mark the resulting Standardized_Item with status `standardization_incomplete`, SHALL record each unmapped field by its canonical name, SHALL preserve every successfully mapped field unchanged, and SHALL route the Standardized_Item to the Review_Queue.
8. WHILE a Standardized_Item awaits a librarian decision recorded by the Review_Queue, THE Standardization_Service SHALL keep the Standardized_Item in the Item_Store with status `pending_review`.
9. IF the Review_Queue records a librarian rejection of a Standardized_Item, THEN THE Standardization_Service SHALL update the status of that Standardized_Item to `rejected` in the Item_Store, SHALL retain the Provenance_Record, and SHALL retain the recorded unmapped field names without modification.

### Requirement 4: AI Concept Tagging and Scale Linking

**User Story:** As a researcher searching for items that measure a particular construct, I want every standardized item tagged with the concept it measures and linked to a published validated scale when one applies, so that I can retrieve items by construct rather than only by keyword.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN a Standardized_Item is created, THE Concept_Tagger SHALL submit the item's stem, options, and scale anchors to the platform's configured multi-model AI router and SHALL assign between 1 and 5 Concept_Tags drawn exclusively from the active Concept_Ontology revision recorded at submission time.
2. WHEN the Concept_Tagger assigns a Concept_Tag, THE Concept_Tagger SHALL attach a Confidence_Score in the closed interval [0.0, 1.0] rounded to 4 decimal places, SHALL record the model identifier and model version that produced the tag, and SHALL record the analysis timestamp in ISO 8601 UTC.
3. WHEN the Concept_Tagger evaluates a Standardized_Item against the registered set of Validated_Scales, THE Concept_Tagger SHALL record at most one Validated_Scale link per item containing the scale identifier, the published citation, and a Confidence_Score in the closed interval [0.0, 1.0] rounded to 4 decimal places.
4. IF the highest Validated_Scale match score for a Standardized_Item is strictly less than the match cutoff defined by the active Threshold_Version, THEN THE Concept_Tagger SHALL leave the Validated_Scale link empty and SHALL record up to the top 5 runner-up candidate scales with their match scores ordered from highest to lowest for librarian inspection.
5. WHILE a Source_Questionnaire is classified Rights_Tier R2 or Rights_Tier R3, THE Concept_Tagger SHALL route the analysis exclusively through the local model lane and SHALL NOT transmit the item's stem, options, or scale anchors to any externally hosted AI provider.
6. WHEN the Concept_Tagger emits its analysis for a Standardized_Item, THE Concept_Tagger SHALL record on the Standardized_Item the Threshold_Version identifier in force at the time of analysis and the Concept_Ontology revision identifier used during tagging.
7. IF the Concept_Tagger has not produced any Concept_Tag for a Standardized_Item after the configured retry budget of at most 3 attempts is exhausted, THEN THE Concept_Tagger SHALL set the Standardized_Item status to `tagging_failed`, SHALL record the last error reason returned by the AI router as a non-empty string of at most 1000 characters, SHALL preserve any partial analysis data without overwriting prior successful tags, and SHALL enqueue the Standardized_Item to the Review_Queue within 30 seconds of the final attempt.
8. IF the AI router returns a Concept_Tag that is not present in the active Concept_Ontology revision, THEN THE Concept_Tagger SHALL discard that tag, SHALL record a validation error indicating the unknown tag identifier, and SHALL continue processing the remaining returned tags without aborting the analysis.

### Requirement 5: Pretty Printer and Round-Trip Property

**User Story:** As a librarian reviewing standardized items, I want to see each item rendered back as it would appear on a real questionnaire, and I want assurance that the canonical record preserves the original meaning so that machine processing has not silently discarded structure.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN the Pretty_Printer is invoked with a Standardized_Item and a target output format, THE Pretty_Printer SHALL render the item into a questionnaire-style rendering that includes the question number, the question stem, the option list with each option's label, the scale anchors when the Standardized_Item's Question_Type is `likert` or `ranking`, and the routing rules when the Standardized_Item's Skip_Logic field is non-empty.
2. WHEN the Pretty_Printer is invoked, THE Pretty_Printer SHALL accept exactly one of the target output format values `plain_text`, `markdown`, or `html`, SHALL emit the rendering in the requested format, and SHALL preserve the source language of the Standardized_Item character-for-character outside layout whitespace without translation.
3. IF the Pretty_Printer is invoked with a target output format value outside the set {`plain_text`, `markdown`, `html`} or with any required field of the Standardized_Item missing, THEN THE Pretty_Printer SHALL not produce any rendering, SHALL return an error indication identifying the offending parameter or field, and SHALL leave the Standardized_Item unchanged.
4. WHEN the Item_Extractor parses a Standardized_Item rendering produced by the Pretty_Printer, THE Item_Extractor SHALL produce a Raw_Item that, after passing through the Standardization_Service at the same Norm_Version, satisfies the canonical equivalence relation with the original Standardized_Item, where canonical equivalence requires identical stem text after Unicode NFC normalization and whitespace trimming and internal whitespace collapsing, identical ordered option lists with matching label and code at each position, identical Question_Type, identical scale anchors with both-absent treated as equal, identical scoring direction, and identical Skip_Logic as ordered source-condition-target tuples.
5. WHEN a Standardized_Item is newly produced and before it enters status `pending_review`, THE Measurement_Library SHALL run the round-trip equivalence check by pretty-printing the Standardized_Item in each of the three supported output formats and re-parsing each rendering through the Item_Extractor and Standardization_Service at the same Norm_Version, completing the full check within 5 seconds per Standardized_Item.
6. IF the round-trip equivalence check fails for any of the three output formats for a Standardized_Item, THEN THE Measurement_Library SHALL set the Standardized_Item status to `round_trip_failed`, SHALL record both the diverging field names and the format in which each divergence occurred, SHALL leave every other field of the original Standardized_Item record unchanged, and SHALL route the Standardized_Item to the Review_Queue.
7. IF the Pretty_Printer or the Item_Extractor raises an unrecoverable error during the round-trip equivalence check, THEN THE Measurement_Library SHALL set the Standardized_Item status to `round_trip_failed`, SHALL record the failing component and the error category, and SHALL route the Standardized_Item to the Review_Queue.

### Requirement 6: Human-in-the-Loop Review and Versioning

**User Story:** As a librarian responsible for the quality of the item bank, I want a queue of items that the AI flagged for review, with side-by-side display of the source page and the canonical record, so that I can approve, edit, reject, or merge items without leaving the platform.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN a Standardized_Item carries any of the statuses `extraction_uncertain`, `standardization_incomplete`, `tagging_failed`, `round_trip_failed`, or `ocr_low_confidence`, THE Review_Queue SHALL display the item to the librarian within 3 seconds of queue load, alongside the corresponding source page rendering and the AI-assigned Concept_Tags with their Confidence_Scores expressed as numeric values between 0.00 and 1.00 inclusive.
2. WHEN a librarian approves a Standardized_Item in the Review_Queue, THE Measurement_Library SHALL transition the item to status `approved`, SHALL increment its version by exactly one integer step if any field was edited during review, and SHALL append an Audit_Log entry recording the librarian identity, the action verb `approve`, the prior status, the resulting version, and a timestamp accurate to the second in ISO 8601 UTC.
3. WHEN a librarian edits any field of a Standardized_Item during review, THE Measurement_Library SHALL store the previous field value in an item_history table keyed by the item identifier and the version that the edit produced, and SHALL retain that history record for the lifetime of the item with no automatic deletion.
4. IF a librarian submits a rejection of a Standardized_Item in the Review_Queue without supplying a non-empty rejection reason of between 1 and 1000 characters, THEN THE Measurement_Library SHALL block the rejection, SHALL leave the item's status unchanged, and SHALL surface an error indication to the librarian identifying the missing rejection reason.
5. WHEN a librarian rejects a Standardized_Item in the Review_Queue with a valid rejection reason, THE Measurement_Library SHALL transition the item to status `rejected`, SHALL persist the rejection reason in the Audit_Log, and SHALL exclude the item from all Recommendation_Service responses returned to non-librarian roles.
6. WHEN a librarian merges two Standardized_Items in the Review_Queue, THE Measurement_Library SHALL designate one item as the canonical winner and the other as a merged duplicate with status `merged_duplicate`, SHALL redirect every existing reference to the duplicate's identifier toward the winner's identifier, and SHALL preserve the duplicate's full record in the item_history table for the lifetime of the winner.
7. THE Review_Queue SHALL support filtering pending items by Survey_Program, by Wave, by Question_Type, by current status, by Concept_Tag, and by extraction job identifier, and SHALL return filtered results within 2 seconds for queues containing up to 10000 pending items.
8. WHILE a Standardized_Item is in any status other than `approved`, THE Recommendation_Service SHALL exclude that item from default search and recommendation responses returned to non-librarian roles but SHALL still surface it to librarians performing review queries.
9. IF a merge operation cannot redirect every existing reference from the duplicate identifier to the winner identifier, THEN THE Measurement_Library SHALL abort the merge, SHALL leave both items in their pre-merge status, and SHALL surface an error indication identifying the references that could not be redirected.

### Requirement 7: Item Storage and Retrieval API

**User Story:** As an integrating service or external researcher with API access, I want to search and fetch standardized items through a stable API that supports both keyword search and structured filters, so that I can build derivative tools and analyses on top of the item bank.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Item_Store SHALL persist the authoritative copy of every Standardized_Item, every Raw_Item, every Source_Questionnaire record, every Concept_Ontology revision, every Validated_Scale registration, and every item_history row in PostgreSQL, with no silent deletion of any persisted record.
2. THE Item_Store SHALL maintain a derived Elasticsearch index that mirrors every Standardized_Item in lifecycle state `approved` and excludes every item in any other state, indexing the searchable fields stem text, option labels, Concept_Tag identifiers, Validated_Scale identifier, Survey_Program identifier, Wave identifier, source language, and Question_Type.
3. WHEN an `approved` Standardized_Item is created or edited, THE Item_Store SHALL upsert the corresponding Elasticsearch document within 60 seconds of the PostgreSQL transaction commit at the 99th percentile measured over a rolling 24-hour window.
4. WHEN an `approved` Standardized_Item transitions out of the `approved` state, THE Item_Store SHALL remove the corresponding Elasticsearch document within 60 seconds of the PostgreSQL transaction commit at the 99th percentile measured over a rolling 24-hour window.
5. IF an Elasticsearch upsert or delete fails after the configured retry budget of at most 3 attempts within 5 minutes, THEN THE Item_Store SHALL enqueue the operation onto a sync-failure queue, SHALL emit a `search_index.sync_failed` audit event, and SHALL surface the backlog count to the platform administrator dashboard.
6. WHEN the Recommendation_Service receives a search request, THE Recommendation_Service SHALL accept a free-text query of 0 to 1024 characters, optional filters for Concept_Tag, Validated_Scale, Survey_Program, Wave, source language, Question_Type, and Rights_Tier, a `page` parameter as a positive integer, and a `page_size` parameter in the closed integer interval [1, 100] with default 20, and SHALL return a paginated list of matching Standardized_Items together with their Provenance_Records and the total match count.
7. IF a search request supplies a free-text query exceeding 1024 characters, a `page_size` outside [1, 100], a `page` value less than 1, or a filter value not present in the Item_Store's vocabulary, THEN THE Recommendation_Service SHALL reject the request with HTTP status 400 and a machine-readable error code identifying the offending parameter, and SHALL NOT return any Standardized_Item.
8. WHEN the Recommendation_Service returns a Standardized_Item to a caller, THE Recommendation_Service SHALL include the source citation string, the source page number, the Confidence_Scores of assigned Concept_Tags in the closed interval [0.00, 1.00], and the version number of the item.
9. WHEN the Recommendation_Service receives a "find similar" request, THE Recommendation_Service SHALL accept either an existing Standardized_Item identifier or a candidate item draft whose stem is 1 to 2000 characters, SHALL accept a `top_k` parameter in the closed integer interval [1, 50] with default 20, and SHALL return up to `top_k` Standardized_Items ordered by descending semantic similarity score in the closed interval [0.0, 1.0], with deterministic ordering for identical inputs within the same Concept_Ontology revision.
10. IF a "find similar" request supplies an unknown Standardized_Item identifier, a candidate stem outside [1, 2000] characters, or a `top_k` outside [1, 50], THEN THE Recommendation_Service SHALL reject the request with HTTP status 400 or 404 as appropriate and a machine-readable error code identifying the offending parameter, and SHALL NOT return any Standardized_Item.
11. WHEN the Recommendation_Service receives a retrieval call, THE Recommendation_Service SHALL require a valid JWT or API_Key authentication credential and the scope `measurement:read`.
12. IF a retrieval call carries no credential, an expired credential, an invalid credential, or a credential lacking the `measurement:read` scope, THEN THE Recommendation_Service SHALL reject the call with HTTP status 401 for missing or invalid credentials and HTTP status 403 for missing scope, and SHALL NOT return any Standardized_Item.
13. WHEN the Recommendation_Service produces a search response under the platform's standard load profile, THE Recommendation_Service SHALL deliver the response within 500 milliseconds at the 95th percentile and within 1000 milliseconds at the 99th percentile measured from request receipt to response dispatch over a rolling 24-hour window.
14. WHEN the Recommendation_Service produces a "find similar" response under the platform's standard load profile, THE Recommendation_Service SHALL deliver the response within 1500 milliseconds at the 95th percentile and within 3000 milliseconds at the 99th percentile measured from request receipt to response dispatch over a rolling 24-hour window.

### Requirement 8: AI-Assisted Survey Designer Recommendations

**User Story:** As a researcher composing a new survey on the platform, I want the AI assistant to suggest validated items from the item bank with citations rather than invent items from scratch, so that my instrument is grounded in published measurement work and reviewers can trace every item to its source.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN a researcher submits a target construct description of between 1 and 2000 characters in the Survey_Designer's AI assistant panel, THE Survey_Designer SHALL call the Recommendation_Service with the construct description and SHALL display up to 10 top-ranked Standardized_Items ordered by descending Confidence_Score, together with each item's Confidence_Score expressed on a 0.00 to 1.00 scale and its source citation, within 5 seconds of submission.
2. WHEN a researcher accepts a recommended Standardized_Item into a draft survey, THE Survey_Designer SHALL copy the item's stem, options, scale anchors, and Question_Type into the draft survey verbatim and SHALL attach a citation reference recording the originating Standardized_Item identifier, its version, and the acceptance timestamp, marking the draft item's provenance as `verbatim_from` the originating Standardized_Item.
3. WHEN a researcher modifies the stem, options, scale anchors, or Question_Type of a recommended item inside a draft survey, THE Survey_Designer SHALL preserve the citation reference to the originating Standardized_Item identifier and version and SHALL change the draft item's provenance marker from `verbatim_from` to `adapted_from`.
4. WHEN a researcher exports a draft survey that contains at least one item linked to an originating Standardized_Item, THE Survey_Designer SHALL include a citations appendix that lists, for every such item, the originating Standardized_Item identifier, its Survey_Program, its Wave, and its source page number, and SHALL render any of these fields that are absent from the source record with an explicit unavailable indicator rather than omitting the row.
5. WHEN the Recommendation_Service is called from the Survey_Designer, THE Recommendation_Service SHALL exclude from the response every Standardized_Item whose Source_Questionnaire is classified Rights_Tier R3.
6. WHERE a researcher's account is configured without entitlement to a particular Source_Questionnaire's Survey_Program, THE Recommendation_Service SHALL still return the Standardized_Item identifier and Confidence_Score but SHALL replace the verbatim stem and options with derived metadata restricted to the Concept_Tag list, the Validated_Scale link, and the scale anchors, and SHALL apply the output restrictions defined by the Source_Questionnaire's Rights_Tier classification.
7. IF no Standardized_Item returned by the Recommendation_Service has a Confidence_Score at or above the configured Threshold_Version recommendation cutoff for the researcher's described construct, THEN THE Survey_Designer SHALL display a notice in the AI assistant panel stating that no validated items met the threshold and SHALL NOT generate or insert AI-authored items into the draft survey unless the researcher subsequently activates a separately labelled control to request an AI-drafted item.

### Requirement 9: Rights Tier Compliance, Audit, and Boundary Enforcement

**User Story:** As the platform's compliance officer, I want every action in the Measurement_Library tagged with the source's Rights Tier and the five mandatory audit fields, and I want hard boundaries that prevent R2 and R3 source content from leaving the local processing pipeline, so that the feature complies with PIPL, GDPR, and the project's own data compliance steering rules.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN a Source_Questionnaire is created, THE Rights_Tier_Gate SHALL classify it into exactly one of {R0, R1, R2, R3} within 2 seconds, applying the librarian-supplied designation when present, otherwise applying the registry-recorded tier inferred from the source registry, and SHALL persist the classification alongside the Source_Questionnaire record before any downstream processing begins.
2. IF the librarian-supplied designation and the registry-recorded tier disagree under the ordering R3 > R2 > R1 > R0 where R3 is most restrictive, THEN THE Rights_Tier_Gate SHALL persist the more restrictive of the two classifications, SHALL emit a `rights_tier.classification_conflict` audit event naming both candidate values, and SHALL surface the conflict to the platform administrator dashboard.
3. IF a Source_Questionnaire has neither a librarian-supplied designation nor a registry-recorded tier at creation time, THEN THE Rights_Tier_Gate SHALL classify it as R3, SHALL emit a `rights_tier.unclassified_fail_closed` audit event, and SHALL block the Source_Questionnaire from any extraction or tagging until a librarian explicitly assigns a tier.
4. WHEN any derived Raw_Item, Standardized_Item, or Audit_Log entry is created from a Source_Questionnaire, THE Measurement_Library SHALL copy the Source_Questionnaire's Rights_Tier onto the derived record at creation time and SHALL reject any creation attempt whose Source_Questionnaire reference has no Rights_Tier persisted.
5. WHEN any Standardized_Item record, Library_Snapshot record, or Audit_Log entry is written by the Measurement_Library, THE Measurement_Library SHALL attach all five audit fields `data_snapshot_id`, `extraction_version`, `norm_version`, `threshold_version`, and `trace_id`, each as a non-empty string of at most 64 characters.
6. IF a write to a Standardized_Item record, Library_Snapshot record, or Audit_Log entry omits any of the five mandatory audit fields or supplies an empty value for any of them, THEN THE Measurement_Library SHALL reject the write, SHALL emit a `audit_field.missing` audit event naming the missing field, and SHALL leave the target record unchanged.
7. IF an outbound API response, recommendation payload, webhook delivery, export bundle, or AI provider call would include a contiguous span of 8 or more characters drawn from the verbatim stem text or option text of a Source_Questionnaire classified Rights_Tier R2, THEN THE Rights_Tier_Gate SHALL replace the offending span with derived metadata restricted to Concept_Tag list, Validated_Scale link, scale anchors, scoring direction, and Question_Type, SHALL emit a `rights_tier.r2_text_redacted` audit event before the payload is dispatched, and SHALL leave every non-offending field unchanged.
8. IF any process attempts to send Source_Questionnaire content classified Rights_Tier R3 to a service whose deployment lane is not recorded as `local_only`, THEN THE Rights_Tier_Gate SHALL block the call before transmission, SHALL emit a `rights_tier.r3_egress_blocked` audit event, and SHALL surface the block to the platform administrator dashboard within 5 seconds of the block.
9. IF an outbound API response or recommendation payload would include verbatim stem text or option text drawn from a Source_Questionnaire classified Rights_Tier R3 to any caller, THEN THE Rights_Tier_Gate SHALL block the response before dispatch, SHALL emit a `rights_tier.r3_outbound_blocked` audit event, and SHALL return an authorization error to the caller.
10. WHEN the Concept_Tagger or any Measurement_Library worker selects an AI provider for a task involving Source_Questionnaire content classified Rights_Tier R2 or R3, THE Measurement_Library SHALL select only providers whose deployment lane is recorded in the configuration as `local_only`.
11. IF no AI provider with deployment lane `local_only` is available when a Rights_Tier R2 or R3 task requires one, THEN THE Measurement_Library SHALL hold the task in status `awaiting_local_provider`, SHALL emit a `rights_tier.no_local_provider` audit event, and SHALL NOT route the task to any non-local provider as a fallback.
12. WHEN any Measurement_Library action is recorded in the Audit_Log, THE Audit_Log entry SHALL conform to the append-only schema of the existing T10 `audit.py` model extended with the five mandatory audit fields and the Rights_Tier of the affected resource, with the actor identity, the action verb, the affected resource identifier, and a timestamp in ISO 8601 UTC.
13. WHEN a Library_Snapshot is created, THE Measurement_Library SHALL assign it a `data_snapshot_id` of the form `snapshot_YYYYMMDD_NNN` where YYYYMMDD is the snapshot UTC date and NNN is a zero-padded three-digit sequence in the range 001 to 999 unique within that UTC date, and SHALL record the snapshot identifier in every downstream Standardized_Item version produced from that snapshot before those versions become queryable.
14. IF the daily snapshot sequence exceeds 999, THEN THE Measurement_Library SHALL reject the snapshot creation request, SHALL emit a `snapshot.daily_limit_exceeded` audit event, and SHALL surface the rejection to the platform administrator dashboard.
15. WHEN a librarian or administrator queries the Audit_Log filtered by `entity_type = "measurement"` for result sets up to 10000 entries, THE Survey_Platform SHALL return matching entries within 3 seconds containing the actor identity, the action verb, the affected resource identifier, the Rights_Tier of the affected resource, the five mandatory audit fields, and the timestamp in ISO 8601 UTC, with pagination applied for larger result sets.

### Requirement 10: Initial Coverage and Volume Targets

**User Story:** As the project lead approving the feature for release, I want a measurable definition of "the bank is seeded" so that we can declare the feature's initial milestone done rather than ship an empty database.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN the project lead invokes the milestone evaluation procedure, THE Measurement_Library SHALL record the invocation timestamp in ISO 8601 UTC as the milestone evaluation date and SHALL evaluate the three milestones defined below against the state of the Item_Store at that timestamp.
2. WHEN the milestone evaluation procedure runs, IF every Wave of CGSS, CFPS, CHARLS, CLHLS, CHIP, CHFS, CHNS, CSS, CLDS, CLASS, CTUS, EASS, ISSP, GSS, and WVS that is present in the configured library source path on the milestone evaluation date has at least one Source_Questionnaire record in the Item_Store, THEN THE Measurement_Library SHALL classify the initial-coverage milestone as `met` and SHALL emit a per-Survey_Program list of Waves with their Source_Questionnaire counts; otherwise THE Measurement_Library SHALL classify the milestone as `not_met` and SHALL emit a per-Survey_Program list naming each missing Wave.
3. WHEN the milestone evaluation procedure runs, IF at least 90 percent of Standardized_Items derived from the Source_Questionnaires named in clause 2 are in status `approved` and the remainder are in status `rejected`, `merged_duplicate`, or `pending_review` with a non-empty review-queue assignment field identifying the assigned reviewer, THEN THE Measurement_Library SHALL classify the initial-quality milestone as `met` and SHALL emit a status-breakdown count; otherwise THE Measurement_Library SHALL classify the milestone as `not_met` and SHALL emit the count of Standardized_Items in each status.
4. WHEN the milestone evaluation procedure runs, IF at least 80 percent of `approved` Standardized_Items derived from the Source_Questionnaires named in clause 2 carry at least one Concept_Tag with a Confidence_Score of at least 0.60 on a 0.00 to 1.00 scale, THEN THE Measurement_Library SHALL classify the initial-tagging milestone as `met` and SHALL emit the qualifying-item count; otherwise THE Measurement_Library SHALL classify the milestone as `not_met` and SHALL emit the count of approved items lacking a qualifying Concept_Tag.
5. THE Measurement_Library SHALL surface the three milestone classifications and the per-Survey_Program counts of total Source_Questionnaire records, Standardized_Items by status, and approved-and-tagged items on the librarian dashboard, with successive refresh completions separated by no more than 24 hours.
6. IF the dashboard refresh routine has not completed for more than 48 hours, THEN THE Measurement_Library SHALL display a stale-data warning on the dashboard naming the last successful refresh timestamp and SHALL emit a `milestone.dashboard_refresh_stale` audit event.
7. WHERE Survey_Programs from the configured library source path beyond those named in clause 2 are present, THE Measurement_Library SHALL ingest them through the same pipeline used for the named Survey_Programs, SHALL surface their per-Wave ingestion status on the librarian dashboard, SHALL exclude them from the percentages computed in clauses 3 and 4, and SHALL record per-Wave failures without halting ingestion of other Waves.
8. IF the configured library source path is unreachable when the milestone evaluation procedure runs, THEN THE Measurement_Library SHALL classify all three milestones as `evaluation_blocked`, SHALL emit a `milestone.source_path_unreachable` audit event naming the configured path, and SHALL NOT modify any prior milestone classification.

### Requirement 11: Researcher-Contributed Questionnaires

**User Story:** As a researcher with my own questionnaire that is not part of the seeded corpus, I want to contribute it to the item bank so that future surveys on the platform can benefit from my instrument and so that my work earns visible attribution.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHERE a researcher with the role `measurement:contribute` uploads a Source_Questionnaire of their own authorship in a supported document format with file size not exceeding 25 megabytes, THE Ingestion_Service SHALL accept the upload within 30 seconds of submission, SHALL classify the Source_Questionnaire as Rights_Tier R0 when the researcher declares open redistribution terms or as Rights_Tier R1 when the researcher declares internal-use-only terms, and SHALL run the same extraction, standardization, and tagging pipeline used for librarian-seeded questionnaires.
2. IF an uploaded file fails the supported-format check, exceeds the 25 megabyte size limit, omits the citation string, or omits the license declaration, THEN THE Ingestion_Service SHALL reject the upload, SHALL return an error response indicating the specific field or constraint that failed, and SHALL not create a Source_Questionnaire record or any derived Standardized_Items from the rejected upload.
3. WHEN a researcher-contributed Source_Questionnaire produces Standardized_Items, THE Review_Queue SHALL surface those items to a librarian for approval within 5 minutes of pipeline completion, SHALL set the status of those items to `pending_librarian_review`, and SHALL exclude every item in `pending_librarian_review` status from all Recommendation_Service responses until a librarian explicitly approves it.
4. IF a librarian rejects a researcher-contributed Standardized_Item during Review_Queue approval, THEN THE Review_Queue SHALL transition the item to status `rejected_by_librarian`, SHALL exclude every item in `rejected_by_librarian` status from all Recommendation_Service responses, and SHALL notify the contributing researcher of the librarian's stated rejection reason within 1 hour of the rejection decision.
5. WHEN a researcher submits a Source_Questionnaire upload, THE Ingestion_Service SHALL require a citation string of 1 to 1000 characters and a license declaration selected from the platform's supported license list, SHALL persist both with the Source_Questionnaire record, and SHALL include the citation string in every downstream API response that surfaces the resulting Standardized_Items.
6. IF a contributing researcher requests deletion of their Source_Questionnaire, THEN THE Measurement_Library SHALL remove the Source_Questionnaire file from project storage within 24 hours of the request, SHALL transition every derived Standardized_Item to status `withdrawn_by_contributor`, SHALL exclude every item in `withdrawn_by_contributor` status from all subsequent Recommendation_Service responses, SHALL retain the Audit_Log entries that reference those items unchanged, and SHALL preserve item references inside surveys that were already published before the deletion request without re-administering those items to any new respondent.
7. WHEN a Standardized_Item derived from a researcher-contributed Source_Questionnaire is recommended in the Survey_Designer, THE Survey_Designer SHALL display the contributor's name and the persisted citation string adjacent to the recommendation entry such that both are visible without any additional click, hover, or expansion interaction.
